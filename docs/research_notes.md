# Research Notes

## Hypotheses

Record hypotheses, motivation, and whether each result supports or falsifies them.

## Decisions

Record protocol and implementation decisions with dates and rationale.

## Candidate Analysis

Record candidate-pool analyses, feature observations, and data-quality checks.

## Experiments

Record experiment identifiers, protocol versions, datasets, budgets, and configurations.

## Observations

Record measured observations without turning them into unsupported conclusions.

## Failed Experiments

Record failed runs, symptoms, suspected causes, and follow-up actions.

## Changes to Method

Record changes to the proposed method and the evidence motivating each change.

## Open Questions

Record unresolved research and implementation questions.

## Reproduction Notes

Record source papers, verified sources, environment details, and deviations from original methods.
